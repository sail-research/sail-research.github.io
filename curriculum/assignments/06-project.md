# Final mini-project and program completion

Complete **one** of the three projects assigned by your administrator: Backdoor Robustness Reproduction, Federated Learning Under Non-IID Data, or Efficient Model Trade-off Study. Read the two planning lessons, your assigned detailed brief and the report/defense lesson. The other two briefs are optional examples.

The project requires a focused question, a primary-source evidence map, a predeclared protocol, a correct baseline, one controlled extension, authentic raw evidence, a 4–6 page report, reproducible code/environment and 5–8 slides. Each detailed brief supplies milestones, metric definitions, essential gates and a 100-point rubric.

Recommended Pass is at least 70 with all essential gates; Excellent is at least 90 with the specified extension and a well-supported analysis. These numbers guide the reviewer; the portal records an explicit Excellent, Pass, Revision or Fail. A correct negative result can earn Excellent. A strong headline score cannot repair fabricated evidence, leakage or an invalid comparison.

The overall program requires accepted work for all required non-project modules plus the assigned project. Reading checkmarks alone do not satisfy assessment. The portal's enrollment deadline governs submission and requested revisions. Keep your shared folder accessible to the assigned reviewer and ensure it contains the final code/configuration referenced in the report.

Before submitting, rerun the README command, inventory artifacts, regenerate one main table from raw metrics and explain one limitation you would investigate next. During review, be ready to derive an equation or recover a reported value from saved counts. Record requested revisions and the evidence that addresses them.
